Privacy Policy

Personal Finance Desktop · Effective date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Finance Desktop is a Windows desktop application for tracking personal income and expenses. The application runs entirely on your own computer. The developer does not operate any server and does not collect, receive or store any of your data.

2. Google user data the app accesses

If you choose to connect Gmail, the app asks for read-only access to your Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It uses this access only to:

  • search your mailbox for transaction notification emails sent by supported banks (Vietcombank, MB Bank, BIDV), limited to those banks' sender domains;
  • read those emails on your computer to extract the transaction amount, date and time, bank reference number and transfer description, and create the matching transaction records in the app.

The app never sends, modifies or deletes emails. Connecting Gmail is optional: the app works fully without it.

3. How your data is stored

  • All data stays on your computer, in the app's local database inside your Windows user profile.
  • The app stores the transactions it extracts. For each processed email it stores only the Gmail message ID, the bank, the processing result and the time, so that the same email is never imported twice. The content of your emails is not stored.
  • Your Google sign-in (refresh) token is encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and stored only on your computer. Access tokens are used for a single sync and are not stored. Tokens and email content are never written to log files.

4. Sharing of data

The app does not sell, share or transfer your Google user data to the developer or to anyone else, with one exception that you control:

  • Optional AI reading (off by default). If you turn it on and enter your own API key, emails that the built-in reader cannot read with certainty are sent to the AI provider you selected (Groq, Google Gemini, or an OpenAI-compatible service you configure) only to extract the transaction details. Account and card numbers and balances are masked before sending; sender/recipient names and the transfer description are included because they are needed to understand the transaction. One-time passwords (OTP) and promotional emails are never sent. That provider's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. You can turn this feature off at any time in Settings.

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking, and the developer does not use your data to train AI models.

5. Limited Use

Personal Finance Desktop's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail: in the app, open Settings and click "Ngắt kết nối" (Disconnect). The app revokes its access at Google and deletes the stored token from your computer.
  • You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the app and delete the folder %APPDATA%\personal-finance-desktop.
