Personal Finance Desktop

A Windows desktop app for tracking personal income and expenses.

What it does

  • Record income, expenses and transfers between your own accounts, organised by category.
  • Monthly reports, category breakdowns and budgets.
  • Optional: import transactions automatically from your banks' notification emails in Gmail (Vietcombank, MB Bank, BIDV), so you do not have to type them in.

How it uses Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. With your permission the app gets read-only access and reads only transaction notification emails sent by supported banks, to create the matching transactions. It never sends, changes or deletes emails. Everything runs and is stored on your own computer: there is no server, and no data is sent to the developer.
